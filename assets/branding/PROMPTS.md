## Logo

Asset type: standalone transparent PNG project logo, square 1024 x 1024.
Create a polished original icon for "mm-nrc-converter", a developer's JavaScript package converting Myanmar National Registration Card formats and numerals between Myanmar and English.
Brand family: Thura Aung's portfolio. Main blue #28518D, dark navy #25313D, optional pale blue #A8C7F5. Modern, quiet, precise developer tooling identity.
Subject: a compact distinctive symbol fusing an abstract ID card outline with two opposing conversion arrows. Two geometric ribbon arrows interlock into a horizontal rounded card silhouette, with a small clean central cutout suggesting structured data. Make the arrow directions clear and the negative space balanced. Avoid generic people portraits and realistic ID details.
Style: flat vector-like brand mark, solid blue and navy color regions, strong silhouette, consistent thick strokes, restrained rounded corners; professionally crafted, recognizable at 32 pixels. No gradients, no shadows, no mockup.
Composition: one centered icon filling approximately 70% of the square with generous clean transparent margins.
Text: none. No letters, no numerals, no slogans, no border around canvas, no watermark. Actual alpha transparency everywhere outside the mark.
